Read me first

Every mill on the map comes from a filing, a press release or a closure notice. Every demand square is a statistical stand-in for the box plants that a free, public plant list will replace. The flows are a solver's answer to "what is the cheapest way to move the board", not a record of who ships to whom.

What a unilateral $70/t increase does to open-market volume

Integrated tonnage does not move: a company's own box plants keep buying from its own mills. The open-market tons are the ones that can walk, and in a market where the next mill is $5/t away, they all do. Real increases hold because the producers announce them together and the index follows.

Mullen to ECT, in board weight

The 1991 change to Rule 41 / Item 222 let boxes be specified by edge crush instead of burst. Lighter liners carry the same stacking load, and the industry's tons per thousand square feet fell. The calculator prices both specs so the gap is visible by market.
