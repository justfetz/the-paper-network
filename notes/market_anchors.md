### B3. Fuel surcharge conventions

- Truckload: add $0.01/mile for every $0.06 rise in the EIA national average diesel price above a base (peg); pegs historically ~$1.20/gal, commonly $1.20-1.50, many 2026 schedules use $1.25. https://www.ooida.com/trucking-tools/fuel-surcharge-calculator/ ; https://freightplus.io/fuel-surcharge-calculator/ ; https://migway.com/blog/how-truckload-fuel-surcharges-work/ (H for the 1c/6c convention; M for base levels)
- Alternative continuous formula: FSC = (diesel - base) / MPG, floored at zero; minority pay a % of linehaul or actual burn. https://enrouteai.com/blog/truckload-fuel-surcharge-explained (M)
- Worked example quoted: "$0.82/mile at $6.20 diesel" (base $1.25, 1c/6c -> (6.20-1.25)/0.06 = 82.5c). https://otrucking.com/resources/guides/fuel-surcharge-rates-2026/ (M)
- Rail, Union Pacific carload mileage-based standard HDF surcharge: strike price $2.30/gal DOE on-highway diesel; surcharge starts at 5c/mile and rises 1c/mile per 5c step above strike; adjusts monthly on a two-month lag (basis month = two months prior). Oct 2026 = 68c/mile (Aug HDF $5.462); Nov 2026 = 84c/mile (Sep HDF $6.291). https://www.up.com/shipping/surcharge/mileage ; https://www.up.com/shipping/surcharge/coal ; https://www.up.com/shipping/surcharge (H)
- Rail, BNSF: mileage surcharge under Rules Book 6100-series; strike price differs by program ($3.25 in grain price list BNSF 90122 Item 2400 eff. 7/1/2026); third-party estimate 76c/mile Nov 2026 at a $2.50 strike (formula-derived, unpublished). https://www.bnsf.com/ship-with-bnsf/agricultural-products/ag-price-documents/BNSF-90122-2400.pdf ; https://steelwheellogistics.com/tools/rail-fuel-surcharge (L for the BNSF rate)
- Rail intermodal monthly FSC example: https://www.up.com/announcements/intermodal/2026/PR2026-57 (H as a source; value not captured)
