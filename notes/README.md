# Notes

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `industry_calibration.md` | Industry balance (capacity, production, imports, operating rates), box shipments, plant counts, company plant counts, logistics, outage economics, prices, each with year, unit, URL, confidence. |
| `market_anchors.md` | Price series points (liner East/West, medium, recycled grades, OCC national and regional), diesel and freight benchmarks, the recovered-fiber loop (recovery, exports, Nine Dragons), end-use shares, corrugator facts, 2026 capacity moves, demand indicators. |
| `plants_ip_sw.md`, `plants_others.md` | What the filings and press say about each company's box-plant footprint, integration rate, shipping practice and outage pattern. |
| `freight_sources.md` | The free public freight sources ranked by what they change in the model (FAF5, Census trade by port, OSRM lanes, STB waybill, BTS Transborder, FMCSA, FHWA, PPI), how trucks per day and miles covered are actually measured, and what stays paid. |
| `plants_by_state.csv` | Every state-level plant or employment number found for NAICS 322211, plus facility-level opening/closure events by state. |

Numbers in these files were gathered by search over public pages on 2026-10-20; each carries its URL and a confidence tag. Where a figure is derived (chained index moves, ratio estimates) it says so.
